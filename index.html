Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding rates, guarantees, and booking security.

How are Ustaad service professionals verified?
Every service professional must submit a verified CNIC, proof of trade certification/experience, and undergo reference checks before receiving customer bookings on Ustaad.
What if I am not satisfied with the repair work?
We offer a 7-day re-service guarantee. If any issue persists from the original repair, the technician will revisit and resolve it at no extra service charge.
Are prices fixed or do they change on arrival?
Visit and inspection charges are 100% fixed as displayed on each provider's card. If spare parts are required, the ustaad provides a transparent bill before purchasing.
How quickly can a technician arrive at my home?
For emergency electrical or plumbing requests, available ustaads in your sector can arrive within 30 to 60 minutes. You can also schedule advance bookings for any future date.
How do I pay for the service?
You only pay after the job is completely tested and finished. Payment can be made in direct cash to the technician or via mobile banking (Raast / EasyPaisa / JazzCash).
